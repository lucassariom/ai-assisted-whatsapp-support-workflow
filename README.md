# AI-Assisted Customer Onboarding Workflow

A practical Customer Success project designed to structure and automate the customer onboarding journey from kickoff to first value.

## Project Goal

The goal of this project is to create a repeatable onboarding workflow that helps a Customer Success team:

- understand the customer's goals and success criteria;
- identify key stakeholders;
- coordinate implementation tasks;
- track onboarding milestones and blockers;
- guide users toward first value;
- document customer context;
- automate repetitive follow-up;
- identify adoption risks early;
- prepare the account for long-term success.

## Customer Success Framework

The workflow is being designed around core Customer Success concepts including:

- Customer onboarding
- Discovery and goal alignment
- Success criteria
- Stakeholder mapping
- Implementation planning
- Time to First Value (TTFV)
- Product adoption
- Customer health
- Risk identification
- NPS / CSAT
- Retention
- Expansion opportunities
- QBR preparation

## Planned Workflow

1. Customer signed
2. Customer information captured
3. Kickoff preparation
4. Discovery meeting
5. Goals and success criteria documented
6. Stakeholders identified
7. Implementation plan created
8. Product / workflow configuration
9. Customer training
10. Adoption monitoring
11. Blocker identification and resolution
12. First Value milestone
13. 30-day success review
14. Ongoing Customer Success plan

## AI and Automation

The project explores how AI can assist Customer Success teams with:

- categorizing customer requests;
- summarizing customer context;
- identifying risks and blockers;
- generating follow-up drafts;
- routing tasks;
- documenting interactions;
- suggesting next actions;
- preparing account summaries and QBR inputs.
